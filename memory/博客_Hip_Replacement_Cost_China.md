## Types of Hip Replacement Procedures

### 1. Total Hip Replacement (THR)
- Replaces both hip joint and socket
- Best for: Severe arthritis, hip fractures, avascular necrosis
- **Cost in China**: $8,000-12,000

### 2. Partial Hip Replacement (Hemiarthroplasty)
- Replaces only the femoral head
- Best for: Femoral neck fractures in elderly patients
- **Cost in China**: $5,000-7,000

### 3. Hip Resurfacing
- Preserves more bone, ideal for younger patients
- Best for: Active patients under 60
- **Cost in China**: $7,000-10,000

### 4. Minimally Invasive Hip Replacement
- Smaller incisions, faster recovery
- Best for: Patients seeking quicker rehabilitation
- **Cost in China**: $9,000-14,000

### 5. Revision Hip Replacement
- Replacing a failed previous hip replacement
- Best for: Patients with implant failure or wear
- **Cost in China**: $12,000-18,000
