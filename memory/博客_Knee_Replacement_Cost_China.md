## Types of Knee Replacement Procedures

### 1. Total Knee Replacement (TKR)
- Replaces entire knee joint
- Best for: Severe arthritis, extensive damage
- **Cost in China**: $8,000-12,000

### 2. Partial Knee Replacement (PKR)
- Replaces only damaged portion
- Best for: Localized arthritis
- **Cost in China**: $5,000-7,000

### 3. Knee Resurfacing
- Preserves more bone
- Best for: Younger patients
- **Cost in China**: $6,000-9,000
