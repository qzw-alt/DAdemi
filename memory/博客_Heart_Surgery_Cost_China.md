## Types of Heart Surgery Available in China

### 1. Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG)
- **What it is**: Bypass blocked arteries using healthy blood vessels
- **Best for**: Multi-vessel coronary artery disease
- **Cost in China**: $15,000-25,000
- **Success Rate**: 95%+

### 2. Heart Valve Replacement/Repair
- **What it is**: Replace or repair faulty heart valves
- **Types**: Mechanical valves, biological valves, TAVR (transcatheter)
- **Cost in China**: $12,000-20,000
- **Success Rate**: 97%+

### 3. Angioplasty & Stent Placement
- **What it is**: Non-surgical procedure to open blocked arteries
- **Best for**: Single or double vessel disease
- **Cost in China**: $5,000-10,000
- **Success Rate**: 98%+

### 4. Pacemaker Implantation
- **What it is**: Device to control abnormal heart rhythm
- **Types**: Single chamber, dual chamber, biventricular
- **Cost in China**: $8,000-15,000

### 5. Atrial Fibrillation (AF) Surgery
- **What it is**: Catheter ablation or surgical maze procedure
- **Cost in China**: $10,000-18,000

### 6. Congenital Heart Disease Surgery
- **What it is**: Repair heart defects present from birth
- **Cost in China**: $10,000-30,000 (varies by complexity)
